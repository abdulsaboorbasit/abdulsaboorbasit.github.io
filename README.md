## 🛠 Tech Stack
[![My Skills](https://skillicons.dev/icons?i=py,pytorch,tensorflow,sklearn,flask,fastapi,mysql,mongodb,git,docker,linux,github)](https://skillicons.dev)

I am a Full-stack Engineer currently executing a focused, 6-month deep-dive into Agentic AI Engineering. My work centers on building autonomous Python-based systems designed to solve complex reasoning tasks across high-impact industries. By moving beyond static interfaces, I am developing goal-oriented agents that can independently navigate domain-specific workflows—from automating complex accounting logic to synthesizing medical research and executing algorithmic trades. My projects are built with a strong emphasis on clean, maintainable code and are designed to be easily extensible for future enhancements. I am committed to sharing my learning journey and insights through detailed documentation and open-source contributions, aiming to foster a collaborative community around the practical application of AI in real-world scenarios.

For my current project roadmap and research, I am focusing on these five high-growth sectors for agentic deployment:

- [x] FinTech & Accounting: Developing agents for autonomous ledger reconciliation, real-time anomaly detection, and automated financial reporting.  

- [x] Healthcare & Medical: Engineering RAG-based architectures that synthesize patient data with clinical research to provide grounded decision support.  

- [x] Algorithmic Trading: Building multi-agent swarms that analyze market sentiment and technical indicators to execute reasoned, autonomous trade strategies.  

- [x] Web Automation & Browsing: Creating browser-enabled agents capable of navigating complex web interfaces to perform multi-step research and data extraction.  

- [x] Legal & Compliance: Implementing reasoning chains to automate contract analysis, regulatory tracking, and document verification.
